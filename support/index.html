Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app signs you in anonymously so you can use groups. We never ask for your email, phone number, or name.

Where is my game data stored?

Players, sessions, buy-ins, and cash-outs are stored locally on your iPhone. If you join a poker group, the group name, currency, and member list are stored in Firebase so they sync across members' devices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I share the settlement with my players?

At the end of a session, tap "Share Settlement" or "Share Summary" to send the results through Messages, WhatsApp, or any app in your share sheet.

How do I join or create a poker group?

Open the groups section of the app to create a new group or join an existing one. Group settings such as the currency apply to everyone in the group.

How do I delete a group?

The group owner can use the "Delete Group" option in the app settings. This removes the group's data from our servers.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app removes all local data from your device. To remove group data, delete the group in the app or email us and we'll take care of it.

I switched phones. Will my history come with me?

Local game history lives on the device it was created on. Group membership and settings sync through the cloud, so you can rejoin your groups on the new phone.

Is this real-money gambling?

No. All-In Poker Manager is a bookkeeping tool for games you play in person. It does not process payments or host any gambling.
