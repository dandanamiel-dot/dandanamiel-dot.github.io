What it does

Buy-ins & cash-outs

Log rebuys as they happen and record each player's final stack in seconds.

Instant settlement

Get an optimized list of who pays whom when the game ends, and share it with one tap.

Poker groups

Create a group for your regular game, pick its currency, and sync it across your players' devices.

History & stats

Every session is saved so you can look back at results and player statistics over time.

Private by design

Your games and player names stay on your iPhone. No account, email, or phone number is needed. Only group settings sync to the cloud when you choose to join a group. Read the full Privacy Policy.
